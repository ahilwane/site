A supplier agrees to take back part of an order. A credit appears on the supplier statement, then a refund reaches the bank. If both are entered as new credits, the books understate the cost twice. Give the return one reference and follow it from the original bill to its settlement.

Start with the original bill

Find the bill number, the lines affected and the reason for the adjustment. Match them to the supplier’s credit note and the goods-return or price agreement. Separate the value of the goods or services from any VAT shown. A payment reference tells you where the money came from, but usually says little about which purchase changed.

Decide how the credit was settled

Consider an illustrative purchase of AED 4,000 before VAT. The supplier accepts returned goods worth AED 600 before VAT. If the original bill is unpaid, applying the credit leaves AED 3,400 before VAT on that purchase. If the bill was already paid, track the AED 600 plus any related VAT as an open supplier credit until it is refunded or applied to a later bill. Match a bank refund to that open credit. Posting the receipt as sales would overstate revenue and leave the supplier balance unexplained.

Keep three dates visible

Record the credit note’s issue date, the date your business received it and the settlement date. They can fall in different months. Where a UAE tax credit note corrects output tax previously charged to the buyer, Article 63 links the buyer’s input tax reduction to the tax period of receipt. Give the VAT reviewer the note, its receipt evidence and the original recovery details before closing the VAT file.

Close the supplier account

In Zoho Books or another ledger, open the supplier’s activity and trace the credit through to its application or refund. Compare the remaining balance with the supplier statement. For an unapplied balance, record who will request the refund or identify the next bill. Check stock records for physical returns and keep a copy of the settlement against the same reference.

One practical month-end test: choose a recent credit and ask a colleague to trace it without opening your email inbox. If a link is missing, add the document or reference to the supplier file. PLS Accounting can help review your bookkeeping and supplier reconciliation.
