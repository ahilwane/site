Make the filing pack a finance-team deliverable

Corporate Tax readiness starts before the return is opened in EmaraTax. A business needs accounts that reconcile, documents that explain the material entries and a record of the decisions used in calculating taxable income. A finance team can make the filing process more controlled by preparing one structured pack rather than sending disconnected spreadsheets shortly before the deadline.

A useful pack allows another reviewer to move from the final return back to the tax calculation, the financial statements and the underlying evidence. It should also explain significant differences from the previous period.

Confirm the entity and the tax period

Keep the current licence, constitutional documents, ownership information and Corporate Tax registration details together. Confirm the financial year and registered tax period before preparing a deadline calendar. Where a group has several entities, maintain a separate status line for each entity and clearly identify any approved tax-group arrangement.

Do not assume that a change in an accounting spreadsheet automatically changes the period registered with the FTA. Assign responsibility for checking the portal details and resolving discrepancies before the return is prepared.

Close the accounts and document the adjustments

Accounting income is generally the starting point for UAE taxable income, followed by the adjustments required under the Corporate Tax rules. Preparing the tax computation therefore requires both the accounting result and an explanation of the relevant tax treatment.

Complete the bank, receivable, payable and intercompany reconciliations. Review revenue cut-off, accruals, fixed assets and material manual journals. Then prepare a bridge from accounting profit to taxable income with a document reference for each adjustment. A reviewer should be able to identify the issue, understand the calculation and see who approved it without reconstructing the work from emails.

Maintain a decision register

The return guide includes elections, accounting information, adjustments, reliefs and additional schedules. The relevant sections depend on the taxpayer’s circumstances. Keep a short decision register recording what was assessed, the conclusion, supporting calculations and any action or approval required.

Examples of matters to assess include available reliefs, relevant accounting elections, related-party reporting and the treatment of exempt income. Eligibility and consequences must be checked individually. A decision made for one company or one period should not be copied to another without review.

Retain evidence that can be retrieved

The FTA requires relevant Corporate Tax records to be retained for at least seven years after the end of the tax period to which they relate. The records should support the return and enable verification of taxable income. Retention is more useful when documents are indexed and readable than when they are scattered across personal devices.

For practical organisation, use folders for contracts, invoices, payroll, assets, financing, ownership and tax calculations. Add a final approved return, proof of submission and payment evidence. Set access permissions, keep a backup and test that the person responsible for a later enquiry can retrieve a sample supporting document.

Build review time into the calendar

A taxable person generally files its return and pays Corporate Tax within nine months after the end of its tax period. For example, a period ending 31 December 2026 would generally have a 30 September 2027 filing and payment deadline, subject to any applicable FTA decision. Registration and other obligations have their own rules and should be tracked separately.

As an internal planning example, a business could assign target dates for the accounting close, information requests, draft computation, management review and final submission. The exact timetable depends on the business; the useful control is a named owner for each step and enough time to resolve open issues.

Before submission, reconcile the return to the approved calculation, confirm required schedules and check that management understands the principal judgments. A complete filing pack supports both an accurate return today and a clearer response if the period is reviewed later.
